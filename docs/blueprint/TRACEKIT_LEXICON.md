# TraceKit Lexicon

Version: 1.0
Status: Living Document

## Purpose

The TraceKit Lexicon is the official product dictionary for TraceKit.

Each important term has one approved product-facing definition. Other documents and interface copy may use these terms, but should not redefine them inconsistently.

> If a term is defined in this lexicon, product documentation and interface copy should use that meaning consistently.

## Product Terms

### Organization

The business using TraceKit and the shared context in which its customers, journeys, commerce, financial outcomes, connectors, and decisions belong together.

### Brand

A market-facing identity under which one or more Offers may operate.

### Customer

A person or business with a continuing relationship to an Organization whose complete story connects discovery, identity, journeys, purchases, refunds, support interactions, and financial outcomes.

### Journey

The complete sequence of interactions connecting a Customer's first known touch to later commercial and financial outcomes.

### Touchpoint

A meaningful observed interaction within a Journey that contributes to the Customer's story.

### Identifier

A known value that helps connect Evidence to the correct business Object, such as a customer, session, journey, click, order, affiliate transaction, payment, or financial event.

### Order

A commercial commitment between a Customer and an Organization that brings together Products, revenue, discounts, attribution, status changes, and financial consequences.

### Product

What an Organization offers and what a Customer acquires through an Order.

### Offer

A commercial proposition presented to a market, including the Product or bundle, price, funnel, shipping terms, Upsells, Order Bumps, and associated Traffic activity.

### Campaign

A platform-specific or channel-specific marketing initiative that contributes Traffic and performance to an Offer. Campaign is tactical and subordinate to Offer.

### Affiliate

A partner that introduces or influences Customers and may earn commission from attributed outcomes.

### Financial Event

An observed change to the financial story of the business, such as a payment, fee, refund, chargeback, commission, cost, adjustment, or reconciliation.

### Connector

The relationship between TraceKit and a business system that contributes observable information.

### Evidence

Immutable source information collected by TraceKit that supports a conclusion.

### Object

A meaningful business entity within the TraceKit universe that has a purpose, relationships, a Story, and a natural Workspace.

### Workspace

The primary environment for understanding a business object.

### View

A focused presentation of information within a Workspace for a specific question, state, or task.

### Panel

A persistent region within a Workspace that presents a related group of information or actions.

### Drawer

A temporary inspection surface that reveals detail while preserving the Workspace's Permanent Context.

### Modal

A temporary focused surface that requires attention before the user returns to the underlying Workspace.

### Page

A routing or implementation concept only. Do not use “page” as the primary product term when “workspace,” “view,” “panel,” “drawer,” or “modal” is more accurate.

### Story

The narrative that explains how an object reached its current state.

### Permanent Context

The business object that remains selected while the user investigates related information.

### Business Context

The selected Offer or business identity that establishes which business data, Objects, metrics, and investigations are active throughout the Workspace.

Business Context is not a report filter. Changing Business Context changes the Permanent Context of the business while preserving the TraceKit Workspace.

### Temporary Context

Information opened through a drawer, panel, or modal while preserving the permanent context.

### Explain

A contextual explanation showing why TraceKit reached a conclusion and the evidence supporting it.

### Universal Search

A platform-wide search interaction that accepts any supported Identifier, detects its likely meaning, and deep-links into the appropriate Workspace and Evidence.

### Universal Forensic Analysis

A platform-wide way to follow a conclusion through its Evidence, Relationships, diagnostics, raw information, and Explain without leaving the relevant Workspace.

### Replay Journey

An interaction that presents a Journey in sequence so the user can observe how context and Evidence accumulate, select a Touchpoint, and inspect that moment.

### Tracking Health

A representation of the completeness and reliability of observed tracking evidence.

### Tracking Diagnostics

Observable signals, gaps, conflicts, and limitations that explain Tracking Health without overstating certainty.

### Profit

The financial outcome remaining after the applicable revenue, fees, commissions, costs, refunds, chargebacks, and other Financial Events are accounted for.

Profit must always be qualified as Estimated or Reconciled in customer-facing product language.

### Estimated

A value based on all currently available information that may change as additional information arrives.

### Reconciled

A value verified against all expected inputs and considered final for the applicable reporting period.

### Relationship

An explainable connection between Objects supported by Evidence.

### Translation Layer

The product language that converts internal complexity into clear business meaning without hiding Evidence, uncertainty, or limitations.

### Progressive Disclosure

The practice of presenting the essential Story and conclusions first while making deeper Evidence and specialist detail available when requested.

### Customer Workspace

The primary environment for understanding and investigating a Customer's complete business Story.

### Order Workspace

The primary environment for understanding an Order's commercial, attribution, and financial Story.

### Offer Workspace

The primary environment for understanding an Offer's profitability, performance, Customer quality, Traffic contribution, and investment potential.
