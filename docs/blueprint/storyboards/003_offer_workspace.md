# Storyboard 003 — Offer Workspace

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for Prototype

## Storyboard Name

Offer Workspace

## Status

Approved for Prototype

## Version

1.0

## Purpose

The Offer Workspace is the primary decision-making environment for understanding the performance and profitability of an Offer.

It combines marketing, attribution, customers, orders, costs, profit, tracking health, and supporting evidence into one connected business view.

It is not an advertising-platform campaign report.

It helps business owners and operators decide where to invest the next dollar.

## One Sentence Test

> “Tell me how this offer is performing, why it is performing that way, and whether I should invest more money in it.”

## Primary Question

> “Should I spend more money on this offer?”

## Primary Persona

The Founder / CEO is the primary persona because they need a fast, trustworthy answer about where the business should invest its next dollar.

The Workspace must also support the decisions and investigations of:

- Product Owner
- Marketing Director
- Media Buyer
- Affiliate Manager
- Finance
- Operations
- Tracking Expert

## Secondary Users

Other authorized operators may use the Workspace to understand the Orders, Customers, Financial Events, attribution, and Evidence contributing to Offer performance.

## User Questions Answered

- Is this Offer profitable?
- Is performance improving or declining?
- Where are revenue and Profit coming from?
- Which Traffic Sources, Campaigns, Affiliates, and Creatives are contributing?
- Which costs are reducing Profit?
- Is customer quality healthy?
- Is tracking complete enough to support the conclusion?
- What changed, and when did it change?
- What requires attention?
- Does the Evidence justify increasing spend?
- Which Business Context should receive more budget?

## Success Criteria

Within fifteen seconds, a business owner should understand:

- Which business or Offer is selected
- Whether it is profitable
- Whether performance is improving
- Where revenue and Profit are coming from
- Which costs are affecting performance
- Whether customer quality is healthy
- Whether anything requires attention
- Whether increasing spend appears justified

A Media Buyer should understand:

- Traffic Source performance
- Campaign and Creative contribution
- CPA and ROAS
- Profitability
- Customer quality
- Tracking Health
- Where to investigate next

Compare Mode should help answer:

- Which Offer should receive more budget?
- Why is one Offer outperforming another?
- Which performance differences are financially meaningful?
- What Evidence supports the comparison?

## Entry Points

- Offer or Business Context selection
- Universal Search
- Executive or marketing workflow
- Traffic Source, Campaign, Affiliate, Product, Customer, Order, Financial Event, or Evidence Relationship
- A relevant alert, warning, or TraceKit Intelligence observation

Every entry point restores the correct Offer as Permanent Context and opens relevant Temporary Context without losing the selected business context.

## Permanent Context

The selected Offer is the Permanent Context.

Changing the selected Offer changes the business context of the Workspace while preserving the same TraceKit workspace shell and interaction model.

The selected business context must always remain clear.

## Business Context

The Business Context selector establishes which Offer or business is currently being analyzed.

It is not a report filter.

Selecting a different Business Context should feel like entering a different business while remaining inside TraceKit.

Each Business Context selector includes:

- Brand, Product, Campaign, or Offer logo
- Offer or business name
- Current Profit
- Simple trend indicator
- Clear selected state

Approved prototype examples may include:

- Bullseye
- ValueRx Individual
- ValueRx Family
- Pete’s Pasta
- Manifest RX

The logo is a first-class identity element.

The application shell remains recognizably TraceKit. Brand identity may affect the logo and subtle context accents, but must not redesign the application chrome.

The user may choose a default Business Context.

When no preference exists, TraceKit may select the first available context.

## Primary Sections

### 1. Business Context

Establishes the selected Offer or business and makes context switching clear, intentional, and distinct from report filtering.

### 2. Offer Summary

Explains current Profit, performance, financial qualification, customer outcomes, trends, and attention states at a glance.

### 3. Traffic Sources

Shows where Offer performance originates and provides subordinate drill-downs into tactical marketing Objects.

### 4. Profit Drivers

Explains the revenue, costs, Financial Events, and commercial behavior affecting Profit.

### 5. Customer Quality

Explains whether the Offer attracts valuable, durable Customers rather than conversions alone.

### 6. Offer Timeline

Connects meaningful business changes to performance movement and supporting Evidence.

### 7. TraceKit Intelligence

Represents the separate future add-on through evidence-backed prototype observations, risks, opportunities, and recommendations.

### 8. Related Objects and Investigations

Provides paths into the Customers, Orders, marketing Objects, costs, Financial Events, Relationships, and Evidence behind the Offer Story without losing Permanent Context.

## TraceKit Intelligence

TraceKit Intelligence is a separate future add-on.

It is proactive rather than reactive.

MCP Chat remains part of the Core platform and answers questions when users ask.

TraceKit Intelligence proactively surfaces evidence-backed observations, risks, opportunities, and recommendations.

Approved examples include:

- Profit increased because CPA decreased.
- Shipping losses are reducing margin.
- Affiliate traffic is producing higher-Profit Customers.
- Refund rate increased after a Landing Page change.
- One Traffic Source is outperforming another.
- Offer margin has declined for three consecutive days.
- A specific Creative is producing better Customer quality.

Every recommendation includes:

- The observation
- Supporting comparison
- Reason or driver
- Recommendation
- Explain
- Evidence

TraceKit Intelligence never replaces Evidence.

## Compare Mode

Compare Mode is part of the approved product direction.

It allows users to compare two or more Business Contexts while remaining inside the Offer Workspace.

Approved comparisons include:

- Offer versus Offer
- Brand versus Brand
- Client versus Client, where permissions allow
- Product or Campaign context comparisons where supported later

Compare Mode supports comparison of:

- Profit
- Revenue
- Spend
- Profit Margin
- ROAS
- CPA
- Orders
- Customers
- Refund rate
- Chargeback rate
- Shipping margin
- Customer quality
- Lifetime value
- Traffic Source performance
- Tracking Health

Compare Mode must not become a generic spreadsheet or dense table wall.

It should remain readable, visual, and decision-oriented.

TraceKit Intelligence may explain why one Business Context is outperforming another and recommend where additional budget should be allocated.

Every comparative conclusion must link to supporting Evidence.

Compare Mode is an approved capability for the interactive prototype. The prototype may use focused mock behavior rather than a complete production comparison engine.

## Data Sources

The Offer Story may use approved commerce, marketing, attribution, Customer, Order, payment, cost, shipping, financial, and Connector Evidence.

This storyboard does not define source mappings or imply that any Connector or Evidence source is complete.

## Data Freshness Requirements

The Workspace communicates the freshness and completeness of material Evidence wherever delay or absence affects Profit, performance, attribution, Customer Quality, Tracking Health, comparison, or a recommendation.

No fixed freshness interval is established by this storyboard.

## Profit/Attribution Implications

Profit is the primary financial metric and is always qualified as Estimated or Reconciled.

Estimated values disclose the known inputs and limitations that may change the result. Reconciled values are verified against all expected inputs for the applicable reporting period.

The Offer Workspace preserves first-touch attribution and the complete Customer Journey while aggregating Customers, Orders, Traffic Sources, Campaigns, Affiliates, and Financial Events into the selected Offer context.

Traffic Source and Campaign performance remain subordinate explanations of the Offer's strategic outcome.

## Desktop Layout

Desktop is the primary decision and investigation environment.

The Workspace uses the shared TraceKit shell established by the Customer and Order Workspaces. Business Context, Profit, and performance lead the experience. Traffic Sources, Profit Drivers, Customer Quality, Timeline, Intelligence, Related Objects, and Compare Mode follow through a clear hierarchy with progressive disclosure.

The layout must remain calm, information-dense, and decision-oriented rather than becoming a wall of cards, charts, or tables.

## Tablet Layout

Business Context remains clear while selectors and summary metrics wrap or scroll as needed. Primary conclusions remain ahead of detailed Traffic Source, Profit Driver, Customer Quality, Timeline, and comparison content.

Temporary Context may overlay more of the Workspace while preserving the selected Offer.

## Mobile Layout

The selected Business Context, Profit qualification, performance direction, attention state, and primary decision remain first.

Selectors, comparisons, and detailed sections may stack or scroll. Compare Mode must preserve labels, selected contexts, metric meaning, and Evidence paths without relying on color.

Temporary Context may become full-width without losing the selected Offer.

## Loading State

Preserve the Business Context and summary hierarchy while required information loads.

Do not display unsupported Profit, performance, comparison, attribution, Customer Quality, or Intelligence conclusions.

## Empty State

Explain which approved area has no qualifying Evidence and what the user can inspect or do next.

An empty state must not imply that an event, cost, Customer outcome, or marketing contribution did not occur.

## Partial Data State

Identify which sources and conclusions are available, what is missing, and how the limitation affects Profit, performance, comparison, or a recommendation.

Partial data must not appear Reconciled or complete.

## Stale Data State

Show the affected Evidence, its freshness, the impact on the Offer Story, and the available next inspection.

Stale Evidence must not silently retain a trusted appearance.

## Error State

Explain what could not be shown, which parts of the Offer Story remain available, and what the user can retry or inspect.

An error in Temporary Context must not discard the selected Offer.

## Permissions

Permissions must preserve the same Offer Story hierarchy while restricting sensitive Business Contexts, Evidence, comparisons, and Relationships appropriately.

Client-versus-client comparison is available only where permissions allow.

Specific production roles and boundaries remain to be defined before production approval.

## Accessibility

- Important states never rely on color alone.
- Logos are not the only indicator of Business Context.
- Every selector includes a text name and selected state.
- Trend indicators use labels, icons, direction, and values.
- Compare Mode remains understandable in grayscale.
- Charts and visual comparisons use labels, patterns, markers, or line styles in addition to color.
- The Workspace uses high contrast.
- The Color Vision Optimized appearance palette remains part of the product direction.
- The experience remains consistent with Customer and Order Workspaces.
- Keyboard and assistive-technology users receive equivalent access to Business Context selection, comparison, Explain, Evidence, Relationships, and Temporary Context.

## Analytics / Product Events

Product measurement requirements will be defined before production implementation.

The storyboard does not prescribe analytics events or introduce additional product behavior.

## Out of Scope

- Live data integration for the interactive prototype
- Production source mappings or Connector behavior
- Production permissions
- A complete production comparison engine
- An advertising-platform campaign report
- A separate Campaign Workspace in this storyboard
- Production TraceKit Intelligence

- Future Enhancements listed below

## Demo Script

1. Open the Offer Workspace.
2. Show the Business Context logo selectors.
3. Select Bullseye.
4. Show Profit and Performance.
5. Review Traffic Sources.
6. Explain a major Profit Driver.
7. Open a related Order or Customer without losing Offer context.
8. Show a meaningful Offer Timeline event.
9. Open Compare Mode.
10. Compare Bullseye with ValueRx.
11. Show the key financial and customer-quality differences.
12. Show a TraceKit Intelligence recommendation explaining which Offer should receive more budget.
13. Open Explain and supporting Evidence.
14. Return to the selected Business Context.

This sequence should be presentable in approximately 90 seconds.

## Decisions Made

- Storyboard 003 is the Offer Workspace, not the Campaign Workspace.
- Offer is strategic; Campaign is tactical and subordinate.
- The selected Offer is Permanent Context.
- The top selectors are called Business Context.
- Business Context uses logos plus text and metrics.
- Changing Business Context is not a report filter.
- Profit and Performance are the Workspace hero.
- Compare Mode is an approved capability.
- Compare Mode is part of Core product direction, not dependent on TraceKit Intelligence.
- TraceKit Intelligence may interpret comparisons and recommend action.
- MCP Chat remains part of Core and is reactive.
- TraceKit Intelligence is a separate future add-on and is proactive.
- The Workspace must retain the same TraceKit look and feel as Customer and Order.
