# TraceKit Profit Engine Specification v1

## 1. Purpose

The TraceKit Profit Engine is the canonical system responsible for calculating and explaining Operational Profit and Reconciled Profit.

This specification governs:

- commerce data
- attribution
- financial events
- costs
- profit calculations
- connector readiness
- reconciliation
- Executive Dashboard presentation

The Profit Engine is not a dashboard-only calculation layer. It is the accounting interpretation layer that connects source commerce, attribution, financial ledger, cost configuration, and reconciliation evidence into explainable business performance.

## 2. Guiding Principle

“TraceKit will never sacrifice financial accuracy for dashboard completeness. If data required to calculate profit is missing, stale, estimated, or only partially mapped, TraceKit will clearly communicate the limitation rather than presenting a misleading profit figure.”

## 3. Design Principles

- Profit First: TraceKit should prioritize the accuracy and explainability of profit over vanity reporting.
- Every Dollar Must Be Explainable: Every monetary value in a profit result must be traceable to a source record, approved calculation, or accepted adjustment.
- Order-Level Accounting: Aggregated profit must derive from canonical order- or event-level records wherever possible.
- Transparency Over Assumptions: Missing, stale, estimated, or partially mapped inputs must remain visible to users.
- Preserve Source and Calculation Provenance: Source values, calculated values, overrides, and reconciliation adjustments must remain distinguishable.
- Idempotent and Auditable Calculations: Repeated imports, retries, and recalculations must not duplicate financial impact.
- No Silent Financial Defaults: Missing financial categories must not be silently interpreted as zero.

## 4. Profit States

### Operational Profit

Operational Profit is continuously updated from live imported and configured data. It is intended for daily operational decisions and may include calculated processor costs when an approved contract model exists. Operational Profit must always expose completeness and freshness, including connector readiness, source freshness, and missing cost inputs.

### Reconciled Profit

Reconciled Profit is a closed-period result verified against processor statements and other external financial records. It includes accepted reconciliation adjustments and is immutable after close unless the period is explicitly reopened. Reconciled Profit must preserve the original operational calculation, statement evidence, variance, accepted adjustment, and closing metadata.

## 5. Canonical Profit Formula

Conceptual calculation:

```text
Gross Commerce Revenue
- Discounts not already reflected in revenue
- Refund Principal
- Chargeback Principal
- Affiliate Commission
- Processing Fees
- Chargeback Fees
- COGS
- Shipping
- Fulfillment
- Packaging
- Advertising Spend
- Agency Fees
- Platform Fees
- Other Approved Costs
+ Financial Reversals and Accepted Adjustments
= Operational Profit
```

Taxes must be configurable as included, excluded, or pass-through. Double deductions are prohibited. Each component must have a documented source. Aggregations must derive from canonical order- or event-level records rather than ad hoc dashboard summaries.

## 6. Business Metric Definitions

- Sale: A commerce row whose order type is `Regular Order`.
- Order: A commerce row whose order type is `Regular Order`, `Upsell Order`, or `Mini Upsell Order`.
- Gross Commerce Revenue: The gross revenue imported from `platform_orders` for valid commerce orders before refund, chargeback, commission, fee, cost, and adjustment deductions.
- Net Revenue: Gross Commerce Revenue minus discounts not already reflected in revenue, refund principal, and chargeback principal. Net Revenue is not Net Profit.
- Average Order Value: Total commerce order revenue divided by valid orders.
- Affiliate Commission: Payable commission generated by the Payout Engine and stored in `affiliate_commissions`.
- Processing Fee: A processor fee imported from an authoritative financial source or calculated from an approved processor contract fee schedule.
- Refund: A financial ledger event representing returned principal to a customer.
- Partial Refund: A refund event for less than the original order principal. Multiple partial refunds on one order remain separate financial events when source identifiers are distinct.
- Chargeback: A financial ledger event representing disputed principal.
- Chargeback Fee: A financial ledger event representing a processor or network fee tied to chargeback handling.
- Reversal: A financial ledger event that reverses or recovers a prior debit, only when the source provides explicit financial evidence.
- COGS: Product cost attached to order-level product evidence or another approved cost source.
- Shipping Cost: Fulfillment or carrier cost attributable to an order or period through an approved allocation method.
- Operational Profit: The current operating profit result from canonical commerce, attribution, financial ledger, cost configuration, and accepted operational adjustments.
- Reconciled Profit: The closed-period profit result after external statement verification and accepted reconciliation adjustments.
- Reconciliation Adjustment: An accepted adjustment that explains the difference between calculated operational values and external statement evidence.

Current TraceKit commerce definitions:

- Sale = Regular Order.
- Order = Regular Order, Upsell Order, or Mini Upsell Order.
- Commerce revenue comes from `platform_orders`.
- Financial events come from the canonical financial ledger/conversions layer.
- Affiliate commission comes from `affiliate_commissions`.
- Revenue After Affiliate Commission must not be labeled Net Profit.

## 7. Data Source Classification

Every monetary value must be classified as one of:

- Imported
- Contract-Calculated
- Estimated
- Manual
- Reconciled Adjustment

Source precedence:

1. Authoritative imported transaction-level value
2. Approved manual transaction-level override
3. Contract-calculated value
4. Approved period-level reconciliation adjustment
5. Missing

An estimate must never silently replace an authoritative imported value. Estimates may be displayed only as estimates and may not be used to claim complete profit.

## 8. Connector Roles

TraceKit connector roles are organized into these layers:

- Commerce: order snapshots, commerce status, product and brand evidence, order timestamps, and customer references.
- Attribution: journey touchpoints, attribution credits, and selected policy evidence.
- Financial: payments, refunds, chargebacks, fees, reversals, and other financial ledger events.
- Costs: COGS, shipping, fulfillment, packaging, software, advertising, agency fees, and approved manual costs.
- Operations: import jobs, task freshness, failures, retries, and reconciliation workflow state.
- Presentation: Executive Dashboard, Financial Imports, Financial Health, drill-downs, and audit views.

Each connector must distinguish:

- sync health
- source freshness
- financial mapping completeness
- profit readiness

Connector profit states:

- Profit Ready: The connector is enabled, fresh, mapped to authoritative financial events where required, and has required cost configuration.
- Snapshot Mode: Transaction data is syncing, but the connector is not yet an authoritative financial-ledger source. TraceKit must not represent the connector as profit-complete.
- Financial Mapping Pending: Source data is available, but one or more financial event mappings are missing or unapproved.
- Not Configured: The connector is not enabled or lacks required credentials/settings.

Use “Snapshot Mode” in customer-facing language instead of “diagnostic-only”.

## 9. NMI Contract-Based Fee Model

NMI processing fees are configurable during setup.

Supported fee schedule fields:

- percentage rate
- fixed per-transaction fee
- chargeback fee
- refund fee, if applicable
- whether original processing fees are retained or returned on refund
- currency
- effective start date
- optional end date

Calculation:

```text
Calculated Processing Fee
=
Eligible Captured Amount x Percentage Rate
+
Fixed Per-Transaction Fee
```

Requirements:

- Fee schedules are effective-dated.
- Historical transactions use the schedule active on the transaction date.
- Only eligible successful/captured transactions incur the normal processing fee.
- Chargeback fees require confirmed chargeback evidence.
- Calculated fees must be labeled Contract-Calculated.
- Future imported processor fees take precedence.
- Calculation and actual statement values must both be preserved.

## 10. PayPal Financial Model

PayPal may provide:

- payments
- refunds
- partial refunds
- processing fees
- disputes
- chargebacks
- chargeback fees
- reversals
- currencies
- parent transaction relationships

Authoritative imported PayPal values take precedence over calculated values.

## 11. Reconciliation Model

TraceKit supports monthly reconciliation for each financial category.

Statuses:

- Pending
- Reconciled
- Variance Detected
- Adjustment Required
- Closed

Store separately:

- calculated amount
- imported statement amount
- variance
- accepted adjustment
- reconciled amount
- reconciled by
- reconciled at
- supporting source/reference

Original operational calculations must not be overwritten by reconciliation.

## 12. Reporting Readiness

Readiness dimensions:

- Commerce Current
- Attribution Current
- Transaction Snapshots Current
- Financial Ledger Current
- Financial Mapping Complete
- Cost Configuration Complete
- Profit Reporting Ready
- Period Reconciled

Operational Profit may only be presented as complete when all required enabled sources are sufficiently fresh and required cost inputs are present. Reconciled Profit requires a closed period.

## 13. Common Reporting Boundary

Reliable Through is the earliest completed source boundary among all required enabled connectors.

Example:

- WowBoost is current through 10:00 AM.
- NMI is current through 9:00 AM.
- PayPal is current through 8:00 AM.

The reliable complete reporting boundary is 8:00 AM.

The dashboard must not silently mix later commerce data with older financial data and describe the result as complete profit.

## 14. Dashboard Presentation Rules

The Executive Dashboard must use:

- Operational Profit
- Reconciled Profit

It must not use an unqualified “Net Profit” label.

Show:

- status
- reliable-through timestamp
- incomplete reasons
- stale connectors
- missing cost configurations
- Snapshot Mode sources
- last reconciled month

Possible customer-facing states:

- Operational Profit — Live
- Operational Profit — Limited
- Operational Profit — Snapshot Mode
- Operational Profit — Stale
- Reconciled Profit — Month Closed
- Profit Unavailable

## 15. Order-Level Auditability

For any order, TraceKit must explain:

- commerce revenue
- affiliate commission
- processor cost
- refund events
- chargeback events
- COGS
- shipping
- advertising allocation
- manual adjustments
- operational profit contribution
- reconciliation adjustments
- final reconciled contribution

## 16. Validation Requirements

Dashboard metrics must be verified against source systems before launch.

Required validation:

- orders
- sales
- revenue
- AOV
- affiliate commission
- refunds
- chargebacks
- processing fees
- COGS
- shipping
- advertising
- Operational Profit
- reporting readiness
- reliable-through boundary

## 17. Out of Scope for v1

Reserved for future versions:

- multi-currency consolidation
- accrual accounting
- inventory valuation
- subscription revenue recognition
- multiple legal entities
- tax accounting
- automated journal entries
- automated month-end close

These may not be silently approximated in v1.

## 18. Launch Acceptance Criteria

TraceKit is not launch-ready until:

- required commerce ingestion is automated
- PayPal ingestion is automated where enabled
- NMI transaction snapshots are automated where enabled
- NMI contract fee setup exists before NMI can be profit-ready
- connector freshness is visible
- incomplete financial mappings block complete-profit claims
- every dashboard KPI is reconciled against source systems
- the Executive Dashboard exposes profit status and reliable-through time
- no unqualified Net Profit label is shown
- audit-level drill-down is available or explicitly tracked as a launch blocker
